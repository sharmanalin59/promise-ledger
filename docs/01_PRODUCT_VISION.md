# PromiseLedger - Product Vision

PromiseLedger tracks management promises made in earnings call transcripts and verifies whether companies actually delivered on them.

Core idea:
- Extract measurable commitments from transcripts
- Track them across quarters
- Verify with financial statements and disclosures
- Maintain a promise ledger per company

MVP focus:
- 5 companies only (KPIT, Coforge, Newgen, Persistent, LTIMindtree)
- Only measurable promises (no sentiment, no vague statements)

Goal:
Build a system that shows:
"What did management promise vs what actually happened?"

Future Enhancement:
Credibility scoring may incorporate delivery timing penalties.
Promises delivered after the stated deadline may receive reduced credit.
